NEUROSENSE is a wearable biosensing platform designed to support autistic individuals by monitoring physiological, behavioral and environmental signals associated with stress and sensory overload.

Explore Technology Learn More
THE CHALLENGE

When stress becomes difficult to communicate.

Autistic individuals may experience sensory overload or heightened stress in situations where communicating discomfort is difficult. NEUROSENSE aims to provide an additional layer of physiological and environmental awareness.

Physiological Changes

Changes in heart rate, HRV, electrodermal activity and other physiological signals can provide useful information about the body's response to stress.

Sensory Environment

Noise and light levels can contribute to an individual's sensory experience. Environmental sensing provides additional context.

Communication Support

NEUROSENSE can translate collected signals into understandable alerts and trends for the user and authorized caregivers.

MULTIMODAL BIOSENSING

Multiple biological and environmental signals.

Instead of depending on a single measurement, NEUROSENSE combines multiple data streams to create a broader picture of the user's physiological state and surrounding environment.

01

Heart Rate

Continuous heart-rate monitoring provides physiological information that can contribute to stress analysis.

02

Heart Rate Variability

HRV measurements can provide additional information about changes in autonomic nervous-system activity.

03

EDA / GSR

Electrodermal activity measures changes in skin conductance associated with sympathetic activity.

04

Skin Temperature

Skin-temperature trends provide another physiological signal that can be analyzed alongside other measurements.

05

Motion

Motion sensing can help identify activity patterns, movement changes and behavioral context.

06

Environment

Ambient noise and light sensing provides environmental context around physiological changes.

THE SYSTEM

From biological signals to meaningful support.

01

Sense

04

Designed as a support system — not a diagnosis.

NEUROSENSE is intended to provide physiological, behavioral and environmental information that may support communication and caregiver awareness. Sensor readings and algorithmic outputs should not be treated as a medical diagnosis or a replacement for professional healthcare assessment.
