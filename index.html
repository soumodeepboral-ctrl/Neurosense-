AUTISM INFORMATION

Understanding autism and sensory experiences.

Autism is a neurodevelopmental condition with wide variation between individuals. NEUROSENSE is designed as a support concept, not as a tool for diagnosing autism.

🧩 Autism

Autism can involve differences in communication, social interaction, sensory processing, behaviour and patterns of interests or activities. The experience is different for every person.

🔊 Sensory Overload

Some autistic individuals may experience situations in which sensory input becomes overwhelming. Noise, light, touch and other environmental factors may be relevant depending on the individual.

❤️ Physiological Signals

Physiological signals can change for many different reasons. NEUROSENSE explores whether multiple measurements can provide useful context, rather than assuming one signal represents a person's emotional state.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is NEUROSENSE?

NEUROSENSE is a biotechnology-based wearable concept that combines multimodal sensing, data processing and caregiver-support features for autistic individuals.

Is NEUROSENSE a medical diagnostic device?

The website presents NEUROSENSE as a conceptual support and monitoring system. Sensor values and algorithmic outputs should not be interpreted as a diagnosis or replacement for professional medical assessment.

Can one sensor detect sensory overload?

A single physiological measurement should not automatically be interpreted as sensory overload. The NEUROSENSE concept therefore focuses on multimodal information and contextual patterns.

Who is NEUROSENSE designed for?

The primary concept is focused on supporting autistic individuals and their authorized caregivers.

Can the caregiver receive alerts?

The proposed platform can provide alerts based on configured rules or detected changes in available sensor data. Exact alert logic would need validation during product development.
